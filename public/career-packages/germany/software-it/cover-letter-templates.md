# Germany Cover Letter Templates

Use a tailored cover letter only when it adds value or the employer requests one. Keep it specific to the position.

## Template 1 — Standard

**Subject: Application for [Job Title] — [Your Name]**

Dear [Hiring Manager / Hiring Team],

I am applying for the [Job Title] position at [Company]. With [X years] of experience in [software engineering / architecture / technology leadership], I have worked on [relevant area] and have particular experience with [2–3 relevant strengths].

What interests me about this role is [specific reason connected to the company's product, technology, market, or role]. In my current/recent position at [Company], I [specific achievement] which resulted in [verified outcome].

I believe my experience with [relevant requirement 1] and [relevant requirement 2] would allow me to contribute to [specific team/product/problem]. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background fits the role.

Kind regards,  
[Full Name]

## Template 2 — Experienced Software Engineer

**Subject: Application for [Job Title] — [Your Name]**

Dear [Hiring Manager / Hiring Team],

I am excited to apply for [Job Title] at [Company]. I bring [X years] of software engineering experience, with a focus on [domain] and hands-on work across [technologies].

In my recent role at [Company], I [achievement]. I also [second achievement], demonstrating my experience with [job requirement from description].

The opportunity at [Company] stands out because [specific reason]. The combination of [company/product factor] and [technical challenge] closely matches the work I enjoy and the experience I can bring.

I would be pleased to discuss the position and my experience further.

Kind regards,  
[Full Name]

## Template 3 — Senior / Leadership

**Subject: Application for [Job Title] — [Your Name]**

Dear [Hiring Manager / Hiring Team],

I am applying for the [Job Title] position at [Company]. Over the past [X years], I have combined hands-on software engineering with [technical leadership / architecture / people leadership], working across [scope].

In my current/recent role, I have [leadership achievement] and [architecture/delivery achievement]. These experiences have strengthened my ability to translate business priorities into practical technical strategy, support engineering teams, and deliver reliable software at scale.

I am particularly interested in [Company] because [specific, researched reason]. The role's focus on [requirement] aligns strongly with my experience in [relevant area].

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I could contribute to your team.

Kind regards,  
[Full Name]

## Customization checklist

Before sending, replace generic statements with:

- One reason you want this company
- One achievement directly relevant to the job
- Two or three requirements from the job description
- The exact job title
- Correct hiring-team name when available

Do not copy the job description into the letter. Do not claim experience you do not have.
